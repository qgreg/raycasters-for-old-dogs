Raycasters for Old Dogs

A gentle practice range for the pointer beam that comes out of your VR controller.

Checking your headset…
  1. Hold a controller in each hand. A colored beam shines out of the front.
  2. Move your hand to aim the beam — not your head, not your eyes.
  3. When the dot lands on a target, squeeze the trigger with your index finger.
  4. That is the whole trick. Everything in VR works this way.
Notes for the person setting this up
  • Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser app, then press the start button above.
  • The headset will ask permission to enter immersive mode — say yes.
  • Nothing moves you around: you stay standing in one spot the whole time, so it will not make anyone queasy.
  • Hand tracking works too. Put the controllers down and pinch your thumb and index finger together instead of squeezing a trigger.
  • There is a live diagnostics board inside showing controller, button, and ray data — useful for checking that a headset is behaving.
